# Container — Rules

## Overview

**Container** is an economy game for **3–5 players**. You build factories that produce containers, sell them to the other players, buy their containers for your warehouses, and ship cargo by sea to the island — where every container is auctioned. At the end of the game, the containers you have amassed on the island are scored according to your secret value card, and **the player with the most money wins**.

There are five container colors: **orange, brown, white, tan and black** (the black containers are rendered in dark slate and called "dark green" in the game log).

**Optional expansion:** for games created with Offshore Island, see [Offshore Island expansion](#offshore-island-expansion) below for the additional actions and changes to loans and scoring.

## Setup

The game is set up automatically. Each player starts with:

-   **$20** in cash
-   **1 ship**, in the open sea (ships hold at most 5 containers)
-   **1 warehouse**
-   **1 factory** of a unique color (no two players share a starting color), which has already produced **1 container** of its color, for sale in your factory store at **$2**
-   **1 secret container value card** (see [End-game scoring](#end-game-scoring))

The supply holds **4 containers per player of each color** (12/16/20 for 3/4/5 players), 5 factories of each color, 5 warehouses per player, and 2 loans per player.

## Your turn

At the **start of your turn** you pay **$1 interest per outstanding loan** (automatic — see [Loans](#loans) if you cannot pay). You then take **2 actions** from the list below, in any order. **Sailing to the island always ends your turn immediately**, even if it was your first action.

### Buy a factory

Pay the bank and add a factory of a color you don't already own. Each new factory costs **$3 more** than the previous one ($6, $9, $12 for your 2nd, 3rd, 4th). You may own at most **4 factories**, never two of the same color.

### Buy a warehouse

Pay the bank and add a warehouse. Each new warehouse costs **$1 more** than the previous one ($4, $5, $6, $7 for your 2nd through 5th). You may own at most **5 warehouses**; each warehouse stores one container in your warehouse store.

### Produce containers

**Once per turn.** Pay **$1 to the player on your right**, then produce **one container per factory**, in each factory's color, up to your factory store limit of **2 containers per factory**. If producing everything would exceed the limit, choose which factories stay idle; a factory whose color is exhausted in the supply produces nothing.

Place each new container on a space of your **factory store** — the space's number (**$1–$4**) is the price at which other players may buy it. As part of the same action you may also **reprice** any containers already in your factory store by moving them to other spaces.

-   Repricing is only possible together with production: to reprice you must produce, even if that means taking a loan to pay the $1.
-   **Exception:** if production is impossible (your factory store is full, or the supply for your colors is exhausted), you may still take this action just to reprice — and then you don't pay the $1.

### Buy containers for your warehouse store

Buy containers from **one other player's factory store** (never your own — the seller may not refuse) and move them to your **warehouse store**. You may buy any number of containers in the one action, up to your storage limit of **1 per warehouse**, paying the seller their listed price for each.

Price your new containers on the spaces of your warehouse store (**$2–$6**) — this is the price at which other players may buy them from you, and it may be lower than what you paid. As part of the same action you may also **reprice** any containers already in your warehouse store, and you may take this action just to reprice without buying anything.

### Sail to a player's harbor and buy containers

Move your ship from the **open sea** to another player's harbor (each harbor berths one ship), then buy any number of containers from **that player's warehouse store** (never your own), paying their listed prices, as long as your ship has room (maximum **5**). This is all **one action**.

If your ship **starts its turn in a player's harbor**, you may buy more containers from that player's warehouse store without sailing out first — this takes an action, and you may not sail away as part of the same action.

### Sail to the open sea

Move your ship from a player's harbor or the island back to the open sea (also from the Off-Shore Bank when the expansion is enabled).

### Sail to the island (auction)

Move your ship from the open sea to the island and **auction its whole cargo** (see below). You may not auction an empty ship. **Your turn ends immediately** when the auction resolves.

### Domestic sale

Return one container from your factory store to the supply (or from your warehouse store if your factory store is empty) and collect **$2** from the bank. Not available on your first turn of the game, and at most once per turn.

### Pass

End your turn without (further) actions. Rarely useful.

## The island auction

All other players bid **secretly and simultaneously** on the entire cargo; the seller does not bid.

1. **Ties:** the tied players each make a second secret bid of how much _extra_ they are willing to pay (this may be $0). If a tie remains, the seller chooses the winner among them.
2. The seller sees the winning bid and chooses to **accept** or **reject** it.
3. **Accept:** the winner pays the bid to the seller, the bank **matches it** (the seller receives double), and the cargo goes to the **winner's island**.
4. **Reject:** the seller pays an amount equal to the highest bid **to the bank**, and keeps the cargo on their **own island**. If everyone bid $0, the seller must take the cargo for free.

Only containers on the island score **according to the secret value card** — for **whoever owns them there**, using _their_ card. Warehouse and ship containers have the fixed values listed under [End-game scoring](#end-game-scoring); with Offshore Island, bank holdings also have a fixed value.

## Loans

The default penalties below are for **classic games**. With Offshore Island, use [Loan default with Offshore Island](#loan-default-with-offshore-island) instead.

You may **take a loan at any time** (even during an auction — it doesn't count as an action): receive **$10** from the bank. You may have at most **2 loans**. Repaying a loan (**$10**) is also free, but only during your own turn.

-   **Interest:** $1 per loan, paid automatically at the start of each of your turns.
-   **If you can't pay the interest**, the bank seizes goods for each loan in default, in this order: **1 container from your island**; if you have none there, **2 containers from your warehouse store**; any container it can't seize there is taken **from your factory store** instead. (If you have exactly one container in play, the bank seizes only that one.)
-   If you have **no containers at all**, the bank seizes a **warehouse or a factory** (your 2 starting pieces can never be seized) — that asset pays off the loan, which is returned.
-   Seized containers are removed from the game; everything is seized **at random**.

## End of the game

The game ends when the supply of a **second container color** runs out. The current player finishes their turn, then everyone scores.

### End-game scoring

Your secret value card lists a value for each of the five colors: **$10, $5/$10, $6, $4 and $2** (the colors differ from card to card). The **$5/$10** color scores **$10** per container if you have **at least one container of every color** on the island, and only **$5** otherwise.

1. Total the value of your island containers according to your card.
2. **Discard** all containers of the color you have the **most** of on the island — they score nothing. On a tie involving your $5/$10 color, that color must go; otherwise the cheapest color goes.
3. Add **$2** per container left in your warehouse store and **$3** per container on your ship. With Offshore Island, also add **$3 per container in your bank holding**. Containers in your factory store are worthless.
4. Subtract **$11** per outstanding loan.

The player with the **most money** (cash plus this net worth) wins.

## Offshore Island expansion

**Offshore Island is optional:** enable it when creating a new game. It adds the **Off-Shore Bank**, public auctions and a bank harbor. Without it, use the classic rules above. This online expansion supplements the existing Container rules; it does not replace them with all the rules of another edition.

### Bank setup

The bank has three cash lots (I, II and III), starting with **$1, $2 and $3**, and three container lots, starting with **2, 1 and 0 containers**. Those three containers have different colors and come out of the normal supply. Each player has a personal holding area at the bank.

### Call Bank: bid or raise

**Call Bank costs one action, at most once per turn.** You do not need a ship at the bank to bid.

-   **Win containers:** offer cash for an entire container lot.
-   **Win cash:** offer containers from your factory and/or warehouse store for an entire cash lot. Only the **number** of offered containers matters, not their colors or sale prices. Ship cargo, island containers and bank holdings cannot be offered.
-   Bank bids are **public**, unlike the ordinary sealed island auctions. Open with at least **$1 or one container**. A raise must exceed the current bid by at least **$1 or one container**, and must be for the **same lot**. You bid or raise only on your own turn; the current leader cannot raise their own bid.
-   With **3 or 4 players**, only **one bank auction** may be active. With **5 players**, up to **two** may be active, but at most **one cash-reward auction and one container-reward auction**.
-   You cannot start or raise an auction for an **empty container lot or a $0 cash lot**. A replenished lot becomes available again.

Cash bids are reserved immediately and cannot be spent elsewhere. Offered containers are set aside but still count against the capacity of their original factory or warehouse store. If outbid, your cash is refunded, or your containers return to their original store and you choose their new sale prices.

### Winning and paying

If you are still the highest bidder at the **start of your next turn**, you win the lot. First pay loan interest and resolve any default; **auction winnings cannot fund that interest**. The winning bid is paid to the bank and you receive the whole reward. Cash goes directly to you; containers go into your personal **bank holding**, not onto your ship or scoring island.

You **cannot Call Bank during a turn in which you win a bank auction**. You also cannot Call Bank on the turn that triggers the end of the game: after calling the bank, production that would exhaust the second supply color is unavailable.

### Replenishing bank lots

Cash paid for bank container auctions, loan interest, and payments to keep your own cargo in an ordinary island auction replenish the bank's **cash lots**. Container bids paid for bank cash auctions and containers seized for unpaid interest replenish its **container lots**. Building purchases and loan repayments do not replenish these lots. Ordinary island auctions otherwise keep their sealed bidding and matching-bank-payment rules.

For each payment, distribute **$1 or one container at a time**, starting at lot **I**, then **II**, then **III**, and repeat. **Skip any lot of that type currently under auction**: its reward is frozen. When paying containers, choose their color order; the destination is fixed by this cycle. The game places containers automatically when only one color remains, and asks only when different colors can be chosen.

### Sail to the bank and collect cargo

As one sailing action, move from the **open sea to your colored bank berth**. Immediately on arrival, click the containers you want to load from your holding. Loading costs no extra action, but your ship still holds at most **5 containers**. Cargo is **not loaded automatically**; you may leave some or all of it waiting at the bank.

To collect newly won cargo when your ship is already at the bank, **sail back to the open sea and return**. There is no stationary loading action at the bank. To take collected cargo to the scoring island, sail via the open sea and use the ordinary island auction.

### Loan default with Offshore Island

The expansion replaces the classic random-seizure rules above. If needed and a second loan is available, the game first takes that loan automatically to cover interest. Any available cash pays interest; for each **$1 still unpaid**, one container is seized, if available.

The **player on your right** chooses the container, using the first nonempty location in this order: **your island → your ship → your bank holding → your warehouse store → your factory store**. Seized containers go into the bank's container lots, not out of the game. This procedure does not seize buildings or cancel the outstanding loan when no eligible containers remain.

### End of the game with Offshore Island

The normal second-exhausted-color trigger still applies. **All pending bank auctions settle before scoring**, even if the leader has not reached their next turn. If two remain, the online game resolves them in their recorded auction order, completing each payment and collection before the next.

Score as usual, then add **$3 for every container left in your bank holding**, regardless of color. These containers do not count as island containers for your value card, full-color set or most-numerous-color discard. Containers still in the bank's unclaimed lots belong to no player and score nothing.

### Playing the expansion on BGS

On mobile, use **Bank** to reach the lower board area. A lot shows the **reward above** and the **bid below**. Click a lot to offer cash, or select your stored containers and confirm **Bid**. On the bank cash calculator, submitting **0 cancels the draft**; it does not place a bid. Ordinary sealed island auctions still allow $0 bids.

The Container tutorial list includes **Bank cargo and your ship**, **Stored cargo for bank cash**, and **Outbid and recover cargo**. These walk through actual board controls, settlement, payment order and pickup.

**Online conventions:** the minimum opening bid, arrival-only bank loading and recorded-order final settlement above describe this implementation where the reference rules leave room for interpretation. Empty-reward auctions are deliberately unavailable. Classic games are unchanged.
